## 🛡️ Building: Cyberdyne Intelligence

**Proactive, location-based threat intelligence for mid-market security teams.**

Large enterprises have security operations centers and expensive intelligence platforms. Most mid-sized companies have neither, yet face the same risks. Cyberdyne Intelligence closes that gap. It watches news, official alerts and public-safety sources around each of a client's sites, decides what actually affects them, and alerts the right person first.

- 📍 **Location-aware:** every event is mapped and matched to a client's sites by distance, with a stated confidence
- 🎯 **Impact-based priority:** *Critical* is reserved for real impact on a client's people or sites. If everything is critical, nothing is.
- 🤖 **AI with an analyst's judgment:** AI scoring backed by rules, so alerts never stop when the AI does
- 🔐 **Built secure:** two-step sign-in, role-based access, data retention and legal holds
